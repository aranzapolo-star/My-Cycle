My Cycle

A private cycle tracker and guide for women, girls, trans men, non-binary people and everyone who menstruates. Get to know your body, track how you feel and understand your cycle.

Please do not use your real name or personal information. Invent a username (for example “blue_moon_27”).

Private: only you can see what you write. It is saved only in this browser, on this device. It is never sent anywhere and nobody uses it for research or anything else.

Important: because of that, the website cannot recover your data for you. Press ⬇ Backup often to download a copy to your own device.

My Cycle
